# Rift Gates

## Rift Gate

A small multiblock that opens onto one of your rooms, or onto another of your gates.

- **Frame:** a 3 x 3 floor of stone bricks, with pillars three high at the far end and an amethyst block across the top. The Rift Anchor goes front right, the Rift Stabilizer front left. Lay the last block and the gate forms by itself.
- **Choosing a destination:** use the anchor. It offers your rooms (old or new) and your other gates. A plain gate is set **once**, so choose with care. Breaking and replacing the anchor keeps its destination.
- **Opening:** there is a blast that throws you back (it never hurts), then the floor of the gate turns, block by block, into the ground of the other side: void stone for a room, grass for an overworld gate, netherrack for a Nether gate.
- **Entering:** walk into the rift.
- **Looking in:** a small crack hangs over the stabilizer. Stare into it for a couple of seconds and a living miniature of the room comes out of it, with the mobs and players inside.

## Greater Rift Gate

A larger gate whose destination can be changed whenever you like.

- **Greater Rift Anchor:** made around a plain anchor with shulker shells. The plain anchor's destination is lost in the making.
- **Frame:** deepslate bricks, with amethyst on the top at the corners and in the middle.
- **Rift:** three wide and three tall. When it closes, it falls in on itself, and when it opens again the floor changes with it.
- **Lightning:** it throws lightning from its frame into the tear (the plain gate does too, less often). A switch on the gate's screen turns it off.

## Gate to gate

A gate can lead to another of your gates instead of a room. It is one way: for the way back, the far gate must be set to lead here too.
