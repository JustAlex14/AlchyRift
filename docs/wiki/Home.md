# AlchyRift

AlchyRift is a rift mod for NeoForge (Minecraft 26.1.2), built on [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX). You raise a crude gate of stone and amethyst, tear reality open in its frame, and walk into a room of your own inside the void. Then you keep it supplied from the world you left.

## Pages

- [Getting Started](Getting-Started): the first items, in order
- [Rift Gates](Rift-Gates): the Rift Gate and the Greater Rift Gate
- [Rooms](Rooms): rooms, names, guests, rules and growing
- [Conduits](Conduits): item, fluid and energy lines, the tuner, sieves and surge crystals
- [Relays and Ports](Relays-and-Ports): wireless links and lines through a gate
- [Configuration](Configuration)

## Requirements

- NeoForge 26.1.2
- [AlchyX](https://github.com/JustAlex14/AlchyX) 0.2.0 or newer
- Optional: Patchouli (the Exile's Notebook), JEI (recipes), Jade

## The Exile's Notebook

With Patchouli installed, the Exile's Notebook is the in-game guide, written by an exile who found a crack in the air deep underground. Its paper changes as the writer goes deeper.
