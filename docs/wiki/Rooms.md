# Rooms

## Inside a room

A new room is a floor of void stone and four walls, one chunk (16 blocks) a side. Nothing breaks the walls. There is no water, no soil, no rain and no night: only what you bring in.

To leave, walk into any wall. It tears open where you come close and lets you out by your gate.

A room keeps running when you leave it: furnaces burn and crops grow. By default every room stays loaded (see `maxLoadedRooms` in [Configuration](Configuration)).

Above the room there is an eye. It watches.

## Many rooms

You can have as many rooms as you want, and name them. The gate's screen lists them, along with your other gates. A room you no longer want can be deleted from that list, with everything in it. It asks twice.

## Guests

A room can be shared with another player by name. They can then open a gate of their own onto it.

## Rules

On the gate's screen, a room's owner decides what may happen inside:

- monsters spawning
- animals spawning
- explosions breaking blocks

All of them start forbidden.

## Growing a room

**Expand**, on the gate's screen, spends Void Seeds and pushes the walls out. What you built stays where it was.

| Growth | Default cost |
|---|---|
| 1 x 1 to 2 x 2 chunks | 1 void seed |
| 2 x 2 to 3 x 3 chunks | 3 void seeds and a nether star |

The largest size, the item and the costs can be changed in the config. By default a room stops at 3 x 3 chunks.
