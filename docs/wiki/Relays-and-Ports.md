# Relays and Ports

## Rift Relays

A relay joins two conduit lines without a conduit between them.

- **Linking:** use the tuner on one relay, then on another. The conduits around both are now one line.
- **Reach:** a Rift Relay reaches 64 blocks. A **Greater Rift Relay** reaches anywhere, other dimensions included. Its crack tears wide open when something passes.
- **Groups:** a relay takes up to eight links, so relays can chain and branch.
- **Removing:** linking the same two relays again removes that link. Sneak and use the tuner on a relay to remove all its links.

## Rift Ports

A **Rift Port** goes inside a room. Conduits touching it are joined to the conduits touching the gate outside (any block of the gate).

The simplest supply line:

- Outside: a chest, a conduit pulling from it, touching the gate.
- Inside: a Rift Port, a conduit, a chest.

Every gate onto a room feeds that room's ports.
