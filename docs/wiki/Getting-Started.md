# Getting Started

Everything starts with ender pearls and amethyst.

1. **Void Pearl.** Four amethyst shards around an ender pearl. The pearl darkens, cracks and splits into two void pearls.
2. **Rift Shards.** Obsidian set around a void pearl splits into eight rift shards.
3. **Rift Eye.** An eye of ender bound in rift shards and crying obsidian.
4. **Rift Anchor** and **Rift Stabilizer.** The two special blocks of a gate. The anchor holds the far end of the door and remembers where it leads, even when you dig it up and move it.
5. **Rift Gate.** Build the gate (see [Rift Gates](Rift-Gates)) and use the anchor to choose where it leads. The first time, it makes you a room.

Then:

- Grow your room with **Void Seeds** (a rift eye told to grow). See [Rooms](Rooms).
- Bring in what you need from the outside with **Conduits** and a **Rift Port**. See [Conduits](Conduits) and [Relays and Ports](Relays-and-Ports).
- Build a **Greater Rift Gate** when you want a destination you can change at any time.

The exact recipes are in JEI and in the notebook.
